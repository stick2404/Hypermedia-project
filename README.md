User Persona
Name: Laura Martínez
Age: 28 years old
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Occupation: Recruitment Specialist at a technology company.
Profile:
Laura works in the Human Resources department of a technology company. Her main responsibility is recruiting and selecting new employees, especially junior developers and university students looking for their first professional opportunities.
She is an organized and detail-oriented person who values clarity, simplicity and professionalism. She is interested in technology and looks for candidates who demonstrate motivation, responsibility and a willingness to learn.
Goals and interests:
Quickly understand the candidate's personal profile.
Review their academic background and educational achievements.
Check which languages they speak and their proficiency levels.
Learn about their hobbies and personal interests.
Find contact information easily.
Evaluate whether the candidate could be suitable for an internship or junior position.
Needs when visiting the website:
Laura expects a clean, well-organized and easy-to-navigate CV website. She wants to find relevant information quickly without having to navigate through multiple pages. She values a clear presentation of personal information, education and language skills.
------------------------------------------------------
Project Analysis – Information Architecture
The website is designed as a single-page CV, divided into two main columns to organize personal and professional information in a clear and accessible way.
At the top of the page, the candidate's full name is displayed as the main title. This is the first element visitors see, allowing them to immediately identify whose CV they are viewing.
The main content is organized in the left column, which contains the most relevant academic and professional information. It is divided into four sections: Education, Languages, Technical Skills and Projects.
The Education section presents the candidate's academic background, including current university studies and previous education. The Languages section shows the languages spoken and their corresponding proficiency levels. Technical Skills highlights programming languages, technologies and other relevant abilities. Finally, the Projects section provides examples of practical work and projects developed during university studies or in personal contexts.
The right column contains a personal information card. This card includes a profile photograph, location, date of birth, hobbies and contact details such as email and phone number. Links to professional profiles, such as GitHub and LinkedIn, can also be included.
Overall, the information architecture aims to provide a simple and intuitive experience, allowing visitors to find the most relevant information quickly.
------------------------------------------------------
Visual Design
The visual design of the website follows a minimalist and professional style. The main objective is to create a clean and organized interface that allows visitors to find relevant information quickly and easily.
The website uses a predominantly white background to create a simple and uncluttered appearance. Blue is used as the main accent color, especially in the header and section titles. This color provides contrast with the white background and gives the website a consistent visual identity. The personal information section, located on the right side, uses a light grey background to distinguish it from the main content without introducing too many different colors.
The layout is divided into two main columns. The left column contains the academic and technical information, including Education, Languages and Technical Skills. The right column contains a personal information card with a profile photograph and contact details. This separation makes the information easier to understand and allows visitors to identify the different sections quickly.
The candidate's name is displayed at the top of the page using a large and bold font, making it the most prominent element. Section titles are also highlighted using blue text and an underline to establish a clear visual hierarchy. The remaining text uses a simple sans-serif typeface to ensure readability.
A profile photograph is included to make the CV more personal and help visitors identify the candidate. Overall, the design prioritizes simplicity, readability and a clear distribution of information, avoiding unnecessary decorative elements.
